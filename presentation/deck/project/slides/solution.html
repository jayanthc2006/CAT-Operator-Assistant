SAY (35 s): "Everything lives in one operator app with nine screens. The top row is the story of the whole deck: data comes in, we keep the operator safe, we turn history into insights. And then the yellow part, the part we're proudest of: we find a skill gap, deliver matched training, verify it from later sessions and adapt. It all runs locally on the device: Python, Streamlit and a SQLite file, so it keeps working without connectivity." Don't read the nine cards; say "every requirement in the brief has a screen" and move on. KNOW: 8 operator pages + the end-of-shift report, plus a separate prototype Supervisor role (5 pages) chosen at login. Covers every brief requirement: task dashboard, seatbelt, proximity, incidents, working conditions, training hub, unusual usage, task-time estimation. IF ASKED "Why Streamlit?": "Fastest way to build a complete, interactive, Python-native app in a hackathon, and the ML stack is Python. A production in-cab HMI would be native or embedded."
