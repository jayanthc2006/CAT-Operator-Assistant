SAY (45 s): "Real sites have supervisors, so we added a separate supervisor role. When an operator passes a lesson, it doesn't go straight to monitoring: it waits for supervisor review. The supervisor sees why the training was assigned, the quiz result, and decides: approve, request retraining, or recommend an instructor, which uses our booking system. The key point: supervisor approval is NOT verification. Approval only starts monitoring. Here Ravi's slope training is 'supervisor approved', 'monitoring 0 of 8', while the two seeded loops are 'verified, statistical'. Only the before/after tests can mark a result verified. The role picker is a prototype: there's no real authentication yet." KNOW: Roles are chosen at login (Operator default). Operator sidebar has no supervisor pages; the supervisor interface runs isolated from operator code. Supervisor pages: Dashboard, Training Reviews, Operators, Outcomes, Coaching & Bookings. Review panel: operator, why assigned (score + standard + evidence), lesson, quiz score, date, assessment (Approved / Needs reinforcement) + note. Retraining keeps the gap open and the score unchanged; the retake becomes a new case. Instructor → existing bookings table. Seeded Ravi/Anita/Priya trainings pre-date review ("seeded history"). Decisions are append-only in supervisor_reviews; the latest decision wins. New training stays at Monitoring 0/8: live sessions aren't saved, nothing is faked. 51 end-to-end + 13 unit tests cover this. IF ASKED "Can an operator approve their own training?": "In this prototype anyone can pick the Supervisor role at login: it's clearly labelled, and real authentication is on the roadmap. What we did enforce is that the operator's own screens have no supervisor controls."
