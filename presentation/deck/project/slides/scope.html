SAY (35 s): "We want to be precise about scope. On the left is what's genuinely implemented and tested: the rules, the models, the statistics, the loop, the assistant and the storage. In the middle is what's simulated or synthetic: historical sessions, live telemetry, the past training effects, today's task list and service history. The app labels these on screen. On the right is the production path: real telemetry, a certified interlock, notifications, a fleet backend, authentication, saving live sessions, retraining and LMS integration. These are the clear boundaries of a hackathon prototype, not hidden gaps." KNOW: Why live sessions matter: until they're saved into the sessions table, a training done today can't be verified (stays Monitoring 0/8). Security today: no login, and Streamlit listens on all network interfaces by default; production would add authentication and bind to localhost. IF ASKED "What's the single biggest gap?": "Live-session persistence. It's what lets the loop verify new training, and it's the first thing we'd build next."
